Tutor Earn Privacy Policy
Privacy Policy – Tutor Earn

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Tutor Earn respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our application.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

- Name and email address.
- Account identification information.
- Account activity and app usage information.
- Survey participation and reward information.
- Coins and reward balance information.

3. Firebase Services

Tutor Earn uses Google Firebase services to support account authentication and data storage.

Information such as your name, email address, user ID, and relevant account information may be stored securely using Firebase services.

For more information, visit Google's Firebase Privacy Policy:
https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4. Advertising

Tutor Earn may display advertisements through third-party advertising providers.

These providers may collect certain information, such as advertising identifiers and device information, to deliver and measure advertisements.

5. Surveys and Rewards

Tutor Earn may provide access to third-party surveys and reward opportunities.

Survey availability depends on eligibility, location, and third-party providers. Those providers may have their own privacy policies and data collection practices.

Rewards are subject to verification and applicable terms.

6. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- Create and manage user accounts.
- Provide app features and services.
- Maintain coins and reward balances.
- Improve application performance.
- Prevent fraudulent activities.
- Support advertising and eligible survey services.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user information. However, no online storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate the application, provide advertising, or support survey services.

9. Children's Privacy

Tutor Earn is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children below the minimum age permitted by applicable laws. Where required, parental consent will be obtained.

10. Data Deletion

Users may contact us to request deletion of their personal information and account data, subject to applicable legal requirements.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
